STI Vio-Log is a centralized disciplinary management platform designed for the **STI Global City Discipline Office**.
The system digitizes previously manual disciplinary workflows and helps reduce paper-based processes, repetitive monitoring, and administrative delays.
### Key Features
- Student violation and incident management
- Secure authentication
- Role-Based Access Control
- Student account management
- QR-based community service attendance
- Digital Daily Time Record
- Community service hour monitoring
- Student clearance management
- Certificate generation
- Real-time communication
- Department and officer management
- Administrative dashboards
- Reports and CSV exports
- Guardian contact management
- Audit logging
- Real-time updates with Socket.IO
- Security controls and access restrictions
- Cloud deployment
### Problem It Helps Solve
Traditional disciplinary workflows can require manual recording, paper-based attendance, repetitive service-hour calculations, and multiple follow-ups between students and staff.
**STI Vio-Log centralizes these workflows into one system**, helping improve:
- Record accessibility
- Service-hour monitoring
- Administrative efficiency
- Data security
- Student transparency
- Communication between students and disciplinary staff
